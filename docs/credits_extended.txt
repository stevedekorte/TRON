SPACE PARANOIDS — EXTENDED CREDITS
================================

A free, unofficial fan homage to the talent and vision behind TRON (1982).
TRON and its characters, vehicles, designs, film imagery and soundtrack
belong to their respective rights holders, including Disney.

Created by Steve Dekorte in collaboration with OpenAI Codex.

Project source repository (TRON Git submodule):
https://github.com/stevedekorte/TRON

3D MODEL ARTISTS
---------------

arabinowitz
  Tank — Tron (1982)
  Used for Clu's tank and the enemy tanks.
  https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/tank-tron-1982-b730d118f54b4a868339a4ddf11e09a7
  Adapted for scale, materials, turret articulation, recoil and destruction.
  Source records: Sketchfab Standard; creator also specifies editorial use.

Shriker1
  Tron 1982 Recognizer
  Used for the patrolling, pursuing and carrier-escort Recognizers.
  https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/tron-1982-recognizer-ecb52608275c40a28d7d9dda1aed54f3
  Adapted for scale, materials, articulated legs, searchlights and breakup.
  Source records: Sketchfab Standard.

JIHS
  TRON CARRIER — Sark's carrier
  https://www.turbosquid.com/3d-models/3d-tron-1982-carrier-model-1811129
  Converted from the supplied Collada model, with embedded textures,
  adjusted materials, lighting and materialization effects.
  Original film design credited to Syd Mead in the supplied readme.
  Source records: TurboSquid Standard listing; purchased asset.

  TRON SUNSHIP — Solar Sailer
  Used for the periodic sky crossing on the horizontal transport beam.
  Converted from the supplied Collada model, with embedded textures,
  orientation/scale adjustments and a separately animated beam.
  The supplied JIHS readme credits Syd Mead, Jean Giraud (Moebius),
  and Peter Lloyd for the original film design.
  Source: user-supplied TurboSquid model and readme.

jvouillon
  Tron 1982
  https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/tron-1982-d7b1e9a03bca4bb6aa636b56ae45ec88
  Used for the drifting grid cloud, extracted from the larger scene,
  centered and converted to the game's coordinate system.
  An arena-only extraction is also retained as a study asset; the active
  game arena uses Daniel Preti's model below.
  Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0).
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Daniel Preti
  TRON 1982 — Games Grid Lightcycles
  Used for the light cycle arena architecture and its gold and blue cycles.
  A red cycle is also extracted and retained as a separate model.
  Source: user-supplied purchased TRON Game Sector+LightCycles OBJ asset.
  Arena and bikes extracted into separate GLBs. The source floor and trails
  are replaced with procedural versions. Runtime adaptations include
  materials, edge outlines, cycle scaling, surface cleanup and smooth hubs.
  The original large OBJ was removed at the user's request; exported
  models, material file and conversion scripts are retained.

The carrier escape shuttle was modeled locally for this project from film
references. Maze geometry was traced/generated locally from supplied
blueprints. The blueprint artists were not identified in the supplied
files; no individual authorship is inferred.

Model credits record provenance and modifications. The models retain their
respective source terms; they are not covered by the software licenses below.
